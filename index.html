Privacy Policy — Play nearby. Make friends.

Last updated: May 2026

Proxply ("we", "us", or "our") is a mobile game app that connects nearby players for turn-based games. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Location Data

We use your approximate location (city or country level) to match you with nearby players. We do not store your precise GPS coordinates. You can override your detected city manually in the app.

Anonymous Play Data

When you play as a guest, we assign you an anonymous session ID. No name, email, or personal information is required to play.

Account Data (optional)

If you choose to create an account, we collect your email address and a display name you provide. This is used to save your game history and allow you to play across devices.

Game Data

We store game moves, match results, and win/loss records to display your stats. Game data is linked to your anonymous or registered player ID.

Chat Messages

Post-game chat is opt-in and requires consent from both players. Chat messages are stored temporarily and are not used for advertising or profiling.

Device Token

With your permission, we store a push notification token to send you game turn reminders. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To match you with nearby players for games
  • To sync game moves in real time between players
  • To display your win/loss statistics
  • To send turn reminders via push notification (only when enabled)
  • To allow post-game chat between consenting players
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a hosted database platform. Data is stored in the EU region. Anonymous session data is automatically cleaned up after periods of inactivity.

4. Data Sharing

We share minimal data with the following services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Expo — push notification delivery
  • Open-Meteo — city name geocoding (no personal data sent)

5. Your Rights

  • Access — you can request a copy of your data
  • Deletion — you can delete your account and all associated data at any time via the Profile screen
  • Portability — you can export your game history on request
  • Opt-out — you can play as a guest with no account required

6. Children's Privacy

Proxply is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of significant changes via the app. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
